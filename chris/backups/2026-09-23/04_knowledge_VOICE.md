# Voice & Style

> Source of truth: `2. Cowork/AI Talent Intelligence/Research/Brand_Guide.md`
> and the linkedin-agent `docs/VOICE.md` (if present, it wins for LinkedIn copy).

## Core tone
Professional · Consultative · Direct · Confident · Helpful.
> "Short, sharp, relevant — like someone who understands the business and
> respects their time."

Speak like a **consultant, not a vendor**. Could this be sent by someone
senior? If not, rewrite.

## Rules
- Short paragraphs. One idea each. Bullets over blocks.
- Lead with the client's situation, then the value. Never with "we are…".
- Data and proof points, not adjectives.
- **No em dashes, no semicolons.** Use periods.
- No buzzwords (best-in-class, innovative, cutting-edge, scalable, synergy).
- No engagement bait, no brochure dumps.
- One clear CTA per message, low-friction (a question, not a calendar link on
  first touch).

## Length by channel
- LinkedIn connection note: ≤ 300 characters, no pitch.
- LinkedIn DM (first): 2–4 short lines.
- Cold email: 50–90 words, one CTA.
- Video/voice script: 30–45 seconds spoken.

## Bilingual (EN + ES)
- **Default: EN only.** Produce ES only when Chris asks for it (or explicitly for
  a LATAM-based contact when he says so). Do not auto-generate ES.
- ES is **transcreation, not translation** — rewrite the hook to land in
  Spanish, keep proof points and CTA identical.
- **tú**, not usted. Reduce code-switching. Same length budget.

## Michael Page brand
- Page Blue #003A8F, white-dominant, clean and corporate. Never colorful/playful
  in client-facing assets.
- For any branded asset (deck, carousel, one-pager), load the
  `michael-page-brand` skill.

## The Chris test (before anything ships)
1. Could Chris say it out loud in a meeting?
2. Is there one line only he would write?
3. Is it shorter than the generic corporate version?
If any answer is no, rewrite.
